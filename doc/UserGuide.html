Overview

The Fixed Asset Management System (FAMS) includes a robust Depreciation Module that automates the calculation, tracking, and accounting of asset depreciation. This guide explains the end-to-end workflow from initial setup to final accounting closure.

New in this version: We have introduced a formal workflow for depreciation runs, including multi-stage statuses: Draft → Approved → Posted → Locked.

1. Configuring Depreciation Parameters

Before any depreciation can be calculated, you must define the parameters for your assets.

Step 1.1: Navigate to Depreciation → Configure.
Step 1.2: Select either Category-Wide (to apply settings to all assets in a category) or Asset-Specific (to override for a single asset).
Step 1.3: Fill in the following details:
  • Depreciation Method: Choose Straight-Line, Reducing Balance, or Double Declining Balance.
  • Useful Life: Expected duration (in years) the asset will be used.
  • Residual Value: Estimated value of the asset at the end of its useful life.
  • Effective Date: When these parameters start taking effect.

2. Running Depreciation

At the end of an accounting period (Month, Quarter, or Year), execute the depreciation run.

Step 2.1: Go to Depreciation → Run Depreciation.
Step 2.2: Select the Period Type (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual) and choose the specific Year and Period.
Step 2.3: Verify the Period End Date. The system automatically calculates this, but you should confirm it matches your accounting calendar.
Step 2.4: Check the confirmation box and click "Run Depreciation".
All generated records are initially created with a DRAFT status. This allows you to review the calculations before they affect your financial records.

3. Approval Process

Once the depreciation is run, a supervisor or manager should review the results.

Step 3.1: View the results on the Depreciation Dashboard.
Step 3.2: Review the "Depreciation Charge" for accuracy against expected values.
Step 3.3: Click the "Approve Period" button. This updates all draft records to APPROVED status.

4. Posting to General Ledger

Approved depreciation must be posted to the accounting system to generate journal entries.

Step 4.1: After approval, locate the "Post to Ledger" button in the period summary view.
Step 4.2: Confirm the action. The system will:
  • Create an Accounting Journal Batch (e.g., DEP-2026-08-...).
  • Debit the Depreciation Expense account.
  • Credit the Accumulated Depreciation account.
  • Update the status of records to POSTED.
Important: Only approved records can be posted. Posting is a critical financial action that affects your Balance Sheet and Income Statement.

5. Locking Periods

To prevent any further changes or accidental reversals after a financial audit or final closure, you should lock the period.

Step 5.1: Once all postings are verified, click "Lock Period".
Step 5.2: The status will change to LOCKED.
Locked records are preserved for audit purposes and signify the finality of that period's depreciation accounting.

6. Reversing Postings

If an error is discovered after posting but before locking, you may perform a reversal.

Step 6.1: Navigate to the period details and select "Reverse Period".
Step 6.2: The system will mark the accounting journal as reversed and update the depreciation records.
Reversing a period should only be done to correct significant errors. Always consult with your finance department before performing a reversal.
